About

awryLabs is the work of Robert Hoekstra — a one-person operation building independent software. The mix changes from project to project: a strategy game, a research library, a small web app. The posture stays the same: solve a problem worth solving, ship something worth finishing, and keep visitors and users out of the data-collection pipeline.

No funding, no analytics, no roadmap to "monetize the audience." If a project finds an audience, fine; if it teaches me something useful, better. Most of what's published here started as a personal itch and ended up worth letting other people use.
