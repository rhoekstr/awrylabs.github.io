awryLabs / Privacy

Website privacy policy

Last updated June 2026  ·  Applies to awrylabs.com

In brief

awrylabs.com sets no cookies and ships no tracking scripts to your browser. The only analytics are cookieless, aggregate visit counts measured at the network edge: enough to know a page was viewed, never enough to know it was you. That's by design.

Personal data None

This website collects no personal data. No accounts, no forms, no profiles, no advertising pixels, no fingerprinting, and no third-party SDKs embedded in the page. It's static HTML. It loads, you read it.

The one exception is coarse, anonymous traffic measurement, described under Analytics below: aggregate counts that can't be tied back to you.

Cookies None

No cookies are set. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Analytics Cookieless

awrylabs.com is served through Cloudflare, which measures basic traffic at the network edge (page views, rough geographic region, referrer, and device type) in aggregate. This is server-side and cookieless: no script is added to the page, nothing is written to your browser, and no identifier follows you between visits or across sites.

The result is a count of how many times a page was loaded, not a record of who loaded it. There is no cross-site tracking, no advertising use, and no sale of data. Cloudflare processes this on awryLabs' behalf; their privacy policy applies to the edge infrastructure.

Third-party scripts None

None. No external JavaScript, no CDN-hosted libraries, no social embeds, no comment systems. Every asset on this site is served directly. No third party can track your visit through a resource load.

Hosting & infrastructure GitHub Pages · Cloudflare

This site's files are hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which sits in front as the DNS and CDN layer (and provides the cookieless edge analytics above). Both may process standard server-level request data (IP address, timestamp, user agent) as an unavoidable part of delivering any web page. This is outside awryLabs' direct control. Their privacy policies apply: GitHub and Cloudflare.

External links Third-party

This site links to third-party destinations such as the Apple App Store. Those destinations have their own data practices. We don't control what happens once you leave awrylabs.com.

App privacy Per-app policy

Each awryLabs app has its own privacy policy covering what happens on-device, in iCloud, and through any platform services (Game Center, StoreKit, etc.). Links to individual app privacy policies are available on each app's page.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change our practices in a way that warrants updating this page, we will. The effective date at the top will reflect it.
